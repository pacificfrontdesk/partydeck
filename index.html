How it plays

🙆

Charades

Phone on your forehead. Tilt down when you guess it, up to pass. Sixty seconds, then see every card.

🕵️

Imposter

Pass the phone. Everyone sees the secret word except one player. Talk, bluff, vote. Find the liar!

🃏

Your own deck

Names, inside jokes, places only your group knows. Share any deck with a link.

🔒

Private by design

Decks are made by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, not on a server. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works offline. When you share a deck with a link, the cards travel inside the link itself - they never reach us.
