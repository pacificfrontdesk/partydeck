How it plays

  • Charades: hold the phone to your forehead. Tilt down when you guess the word, tilt up to pass. Sixty seconds, then see every card you got.
  • Imposter: pass the phone around. Everyone sees the secret word except one player. Talk, bluff, then vote.
  • Your own deck: names, inside jokes, places only your group knows.

Private by design

Decks are made by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, not on a server. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works offline. When you share a deck with a link, the cards travel inside the link itself - they never reach us.
