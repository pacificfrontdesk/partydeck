Privacy Policy

last updated 8 October 2026

Who we are

Party Deck is published by Oleg Nochka, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Oleg Nochka is the person responsible for the app and for this policy.

The short version

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and nothing about you is ever sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • Decks you make, write, edit or receive from friends
  • Your settings

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never see any of it.

AI decks

AI decks are made by Apple's on-device model. The topic you type and the cards it makes stay on your iPhone; they are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Shared deck links

When you share a deck, its title and cards are packed into the part of the link after the "#" sign. Browsers never send that part to a web server, so even when a link opens our web page, the page reads the deck inside your browser and the cards never reach us. Anyone who has the link can read the deck, so share it only with people you mean to.

Purchases

The optional unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

Children

Since Party Deck collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from a child either.

Changes

If this ever changes - if a future version collects anything at all - this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
